    About
    Me

    I'm a graphic designer with three years of learning, practice, and creative exploration across design, brand development, and visual communication.

    I began my professional journey working for a Saudi company for six months in image editing. Since then, I've focused on developing my creative skills and building self-initiated projects that bring together art direction, brand design, marketing, and content creation.

    I approach design as more than making visuals look good. I care about understanding the audience, communicating the right message, and finding creative solutions to visual problems.

    AI is an essential part of my creative workflow, helping me explore possibilities and produce visual assets more efficiently. But the direction, decisions, and final judgment remain mine.

    Photography is another interest that shapes how I observe light, composition, and visual storytelling.
